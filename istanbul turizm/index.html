Eat like a local.

Istanbul's food scene is an essential part of the travel experience.

🥙

Döner

Slow-roasted seasoned meat served in bread or a wrap.

🥐

Turkish Breakfast

Cheese, olives, eggs, bread and many small dishes to share.

🍯

Layers of pastry, nuts and syrup — a classic Turkish dessert.

🥪

Getting Around

Use public transport, ferries and Istanbulkart to move around the city easily.
